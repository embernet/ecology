## What is Biological Recording?

A biological record is simply a note of what you saw, where you saw it, and when. Real scientists and conservationists use these records to track how wildlife is doing across the country!

Every good record needs four things:
- **Who:** The name of the observer (That's you!)
- **What:** The name of the species you found
- **Where:** The location (as accurately as possible)
- **When:** The date and time
