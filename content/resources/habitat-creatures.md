# Habitat Creatures

<Creature id={`n300`} title={`Common frog`} emoji="🐾">
Frogs lay their jelly-like eggs (spawn) in the still water. The tadpoles grow underwater before developing legs to hop onto land.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n301`} title={`Great diving beetle`} emoji="🐾">
Breathes underwater by trapping a bubble of air under its wing cases.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n302`} title={`Smooth newt`} emoji="🐾">
Can absorb oxygen directly through its moist skin while underwater.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n303`} title={`Woodpecker`} emoji="🐾">
Uses its long, sticky tongue to pull insects out of holes it drills in tree bark. Nests safely high up in tree trunks.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n304`} title={`Eurasian badger`} emoji="🐾">
Digs complex underground tunnel systems called setts to stay safe and warm.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n305`} title={`Tawny owl`} emoji="🐾">
Has specialized feathers with soft edges for completely silent flight to catch prey in the dark.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n306`} title={`Hazel dormouse`} emoji="🐾">
Uses the continuous line of branches to travel safely above ground, finding berries and nuts without being seen by predators.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n307`} title={`European hedgehog`} emoji="🐾">
Rolls into a tight ball of sharp spines to protect itself from predators.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n308`} title={`Yellowhammer`} emoji="🐾">
Builds well-hidden cup nests deep within the thorny brambles to keep its chicks safe.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n309`} title={`Stag beetle`} emoji="🐾">
The larvae spend several years living inside the dead wood, eating the rotting timber until they are ready to emerge as adult beetles.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n310`} title={`Woodlouse`} emoji="🐾">
Has a tough segmented exoskeleton and thrives in the damp, dark microclimate of rotting wood.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n311`} title={`Centipede`} emoji="🐾">
A fast predator that hunts smaller insects and grubs hiding in the crevices of the dead log.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n312`} title={`Purple hairstreak butterfly`} emoji="🐾">
Spends almost its entire life high in the oak canopy. The caterpillars feed exclusively on oak leaves.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n313`} title={`Eurasian jay`} emoji="🐾">
Caches (buries) thousands of acorns in the ground for winter food, helping plant new oak trees.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n314`} title={`Gall wasp`} emoji="🐾">
Lays eggs in oak leaves, causing the tree to grow protective round 'galls' that shelter the growing larvae.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n315`} title={`Polar Bear`} emoji="🐾">
Uses the vast ice floes as a platform to hunt seals when they surface to breathe. Thick blubber and hollow hair keep it warm.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n316`} title={`Ringed seal`} emoji="🐾">
Uses strong claws to scratch and keep breathing holes open in the thick ice all winter.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n317`} title={`Arctic fox`} emoji="🐾">
Often follows polar bears out onto the sea ice to scavenge leftover food during the harsh winter.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n318`} title={`Arctic Fox`} emoji="🐾">
Changes its coat colour from brown in summer to thick white in winter for camouflage. Has a compact body to retain heat.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n319`} title={`Reindeer`} emoji="🐾">
Has wide, scoop-like hooves to walk easily on snow and dig down to eat buried lichen.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n320`} title={`Snowy owl`} emoji="🐾">
Its thick white feathers provide perfect camouflage against the snow and keep it warm in freezing winds.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n321`} title={`Ringed Seal`} emoji="🐾">
Uses strong claws on its front flippers to hollow out a den (lair) inside the snowdrift to safely hide its pup from polar bears.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n322`} title={`Stoat`} emoji="🐾">
Changes its brown summer coat to pure white in winter for camouflage while hunting in the snow.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n323`} title={`Snow vole`} emoji="🐾">
Builds extensive tunnel networks under the snowpack to find roots and stay hidden from owls.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n324`} title={`Narwhal`} emoji="🐾">
Has no dorsal fin on its back, allowing it to swim smoothly just below the thick ice without getting stuck.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n325`} title={`Narwhal`} emoji="🐾">
Uses its long tusk as a highly sensitive sensory organ to navigate and find food in freezing, dark waters.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n326`} title={`Beluga whale`} emoji="🐾">
Lacks a dorsal fin on its back, allowing it to swim smoothly right up against the underside of the ice sheets.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n327`} title={`Snowy Owl`} emoji="🐾">
Nests on the bare ground of these raised mounds, giving it a clear 360-degree view to spot predators and hunt lemmings.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n328`} title={`Arctic hare`} emoji="🐾">
Has heavily padded, large hind legs for running fast over snow to escape predators without sinking.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n329`} title={`Ptarmigan`} emoji="🐾">
A hardy bird with heavily feathered feet that act exactly like snowshoes in deep winter snow.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n330`} title={`Fennec Fox`} emoji="🐾">
Has enormous ears that act like radiators to let body heat escape, and fur on the bottom of its paws to protect them from scorching sand.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n331`} title={`Fennec fox`} emoji="🐾">
Has huge ears that act like radiators to release body heat, and help it listen for prey moving underground.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n332`} title={`Sandfish skink`} emoji="🐾">
A smooth lizard that can literally 'swim' through loose sand to escape the hot sun and hide.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n333`} title={`Dromedary Camel`} emoji="🐾">
Stores fat (not water) in its hump to survive long journeys. Has long eyelashes and closing nostrils to keep out blowing sand.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n334`} title={`Namib Desert beetle`} emoji="🐾">
Stands on its head to collect morning fog on its bumpy back, which drips down to its mouth as drinking water.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n335`} title={`Arabian oryx`} emoji="🐾">
Has a bright white coat to reflect the intense sun and wide hooves for walking on soft sand dunes.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n336`} title={`Thorny Devil`} emoji="🐾">
Covered in spikes that collect tiny drops of morning dew. The water then travels through microscopic grooves straight to its mouth.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n337`} title={`Kangaroo rat`} emoji="🐾">
Never needs to drink liquid water; it gets all the moisture it needs to survive from dry seeds.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n338`} title={`Roadrunner`} emoji="🐾">
A fast-running bird that is quick enough to hunt and eat venomous snakes and scorpions in the scrubland.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n339`} title={`Sidewinder Rattlesnake`} emoji="🐾">
Moves in a unique sideways motion (J-shape) so only two small points of its body touch the burning sand at any one time.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n340`} title={`Fringe-toed lizard`} emoji="🐾">
Has scale 'fringes' on its toes that let it run quickly across loose, scorching sand without sinking in.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n341`} title={`Antlion`} emoji="🐾">
Digs clever pitfall traps in the loose sand to catch unwary insects that slip down the sides.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n342`} title={`Desert Tortoise`} emoji="🐾">
Spends up to 95% of its life underground to escape extreme heat. Can store a large amount of water in its bladder for months.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n343`} title={`Gila monster`} emoji="🐾">
Spends over 90% of its life hidden in cool underground burrows to escape the extreme desert heat.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n344`} title={`Desert tarantula`} emoji="🐾">
Lines its deep burrow with silk to keep the walls stable and maintain a cool, humid microclimate.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n345`} title={`Three-toed Sloth`} emoji="🐾">
Moves incredibly slowly to avoid being seen by eagles. Its fur even grows green algae, providing perfect camouflage among the leaves.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n346`} title={`Scarlet macaw`} emoji="🐾">
Uses its incredibly powerful, curved beak to crack open hard rainforest nuts and seeds that other animals can't eat.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n347`} title={`Spider monkey`} emoji="🐾">
Has a prehensile (gripping) tail that acts like a fifth hand for swinging safely through the high branches.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n348`} title={`Clownfish`} emoji="🐾">
Develops a thick layer of mucus on its skin to protect it from the stinging tentacles of the sea anemone, giving it a safe, hidden home (commensalism).
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n349`} title={`Parrotfish`} emoji="🐾">
Uses its beak-like teeth to scrape algae off coral. It digests the algae and poops out the coral as fine white sand!
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n350`} title={`Moray eel`} emoji="🐾">
Hides its long body inside narrow coral crevices, waiting to ambush passing prey with its sharp teeth.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n351`} title={`Anglerfish`} emoji="🐾">
Uses a glowing lure on its head (created by bioluminescent bacteria) to attract curious prey straight into its massive, sharp-toothed mouth in the dark.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n352`} title={`Giant isopod`} emoji="🐾">
A deep-sea scavenger with a rigid, tough exoskeleton to survive the immense crushing pressure of the abyss.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n353`} title={`Vampire squid`} emoji="🐾">

</Creature>

<Creature id={`n354`} title={`Cheetah`} emoji="🐾">

</Creature>

<Creature id={`n355`} title={`Giraffe`} emoji="🐾">
Has a uniquely long neck to reach nutritious leaves on tall acacia trees that grazing animals below cannot reach.
</Creature>

<Creature id={`n356`} title={`African elephant`} emoji="🐾">

</Creature>

<Creature id={`n357`} title={`Snow Leopard`} emoji="🐾">

</Creature>

<Creature id={`n359`} title={`Himalayan monal`} emoji="🐾">
